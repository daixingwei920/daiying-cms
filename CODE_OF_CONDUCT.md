# Code Of Conduct

Daiying CMS aims to be a practical and respectful project for CMS users, site owners, and developers.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep issue reports factual and reproducible.
- Respect user privacy and production data.
- Discuss security-sensitive issues responsibly.
- Avoid harassment, insults, spam, and unrelated promotion.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Publishing secrets, credentials, private keys, or production data.
- Sharing exploit details publicly before maintainers can assess the report.
- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or abusive language.
- Repeated low-quality promotional comments or unrelated links.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its users.
