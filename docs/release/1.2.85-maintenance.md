## Publication and rollback gates

Push the reviewed candidate and PR. Before official publication, follow
`scripts/release/preflight`, `build`, server-side `sign`, `verify`, and the manual
`publish` boundary. Reconfirm current origin/main, freeze the exact source commit,
verify complete installer/update manifests and SHA-256, perform staging upgrade
and rollback/recovery checks, back up affected publication state, and obtain the
required human READY_TO_PUBLISH confirmation. Never present an unsigned local
candidate or successful Git push as an available signed CMS update.

Production sites are a separate upgrade action and require their own backup,
native dry-run, authorization and health verification. Preserve existing content,
instance configuration, theme files and redirects during upgrade and rollback.
